Can I use my 20 km time to compare my two halves?

The sections have different lengths. A 20 km opening leaves 22.195 km. At 6:00/km, those sections take 2:00:00 and 2:13:10.20 even though the pace is identical. For a comparison of two equal halves, use the timing mat at 21.0975 km.

How do I compare actual half-marathon splits?

Subtract the halfway elapsed time from the finish time to get the second half. For a 4:00:00 finish and a 1:59:00 halfway split, the second half is 2:01:00. Both sections cover 21.0975 km, and the second takes two minutes longer.

At 20 km in 1:54:00, continuing at 5:42/km adds 375.345 seconds over the next 1.0975 km. Estimated halfway becomes 2:00:15.35. This is a projection using an assumed pace, while an actual halfway timestamp records what happened.
