Marathon Pace Change: How Much Time Will You Save?

Running 5 seconds per kilometer faster after 30 km saves 60.975 seconds over the remaining 12.195 km. Multiply 5 by 12.195. The same change over the whole marathon saves 210.975 seconds.

How much faster must I run to save one minute after 35 km?

There are 7.195 km left. Divide 60 seconds by 7.195 for 8.34 seconds per kilometer, rounded to two decimals. A pace of 6:00/km would need to become about 5:51.66/km for that segment to save a minute. This is an arithmetic requirement; whether you can sustain it depends on your race.

What if I speed up and also stop?

Subtract extra stop time from the calculated saving. Five seconds per kilometer over 12.195 km saves 60.975 seconds. With an additional 20-second stop, the net saving becomes 40.975 seconds. If the same stop occurs in both plans, it cancels out in the comparison.

Can I multiply seconds per mile by kilometers?

Convert the distance first. The final 12.195 km equal about 7.57762 miles. Five seconds per mile therefore saves about 37.89 seconds, while five seconds per kilometer saves 60.975 seconds. Use the unit that matches your pace adjustment.
