Marathon Aid Station Pace Chart: Budget Time for Stops

For a 4:00 marathon with eight 15-second stops, budget 2:00 at aid stations and 3:58:00 for running. Over 42.195 km, that means 5:38.43/km or 9:04.65/mi while running.

Marathon pace by stop budget

Subtract stop seconds from your goal time, then divide by 42.195 km. Multiply seconds per kilometer by 1.609344 for seconds per mile. Displayed paces are rounded to hundredths.

Check your elapsed clock at halfway

Half of 3:58:00 is 1:59:00. With four 15-second stops before halfway, the elapsed target is 2:00:00. If six stops occur before halfway and two after, your halfway clock reads 2:00:30 at the same running pace. Both plans still total 4:00:00 at the finish.

How do I count walking through a station?

Use the extra time over your usual pace if you cover distance while walking. At the 4-hour running pace above, 50 meters normally takes 16.92 seconds. Walking it in 30 seconds adds 13.08 seconds to the race clock. A stationary 30-second stop adds all 30 seconds.

Will the pace group stop with me?

Ask the specific pacer how they handle stations. If you use your own budget, compare your elapsed clock with course markers and include the time already spent at stops.
