What if I hold my original pace?

At 2:51:38, holding the original 4-hour pace of 14,400 ÷ 42.195 seconds per kilometer over the final 12.195 km projects a finish near 4:01:00. Finishing in 4:00:00 instead requires about 4.90 fewer seconds per kilometer. Use that difference to judge whether to keep the original goal or revise it.

What if my watch says 30.4 km at the 30 km marker?

Use the course marker and 12.195 km remaining. Subtracting your watch distance from the marathon distance gives 11.795 km and suggests 5:47.77/km. At that average over the actual remaining distance, you would finish roughly 2 minutes 19 seconds after the goal.

What if I expect one more 15-second stop?

Subtract those 15 seconds before dividing. In the 2:51:38 example, 4,087 ÷ 12.195 gives 5:35.14/km while moving. The first table gives the average including any time spent stopped.
