How much does rounding the distance to two kilometers miss?

At 6:00/km, two kilometers take 12:00. The remaining 195 meters take another 1:10.20. A projection based on two kilometers would therefore be 70.20 seconds optimistic. At 5:00/km the omitted section takes 58.50 seconds.
