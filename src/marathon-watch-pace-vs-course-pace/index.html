Marathon Watch Pace vs. Course Pace

If the official 15 km to 20 km segment takes 28:20 and your watch records 5.10 km, your course pace is 5:40/km and your watch pace is 5:33.33/km. Divide the same 1,700 seconds by 5.00 km and 5.10 km respectively.

Compare watch and course pace over one segment

At each official marker, note elapsed time and watch distance. Subtract the first reading from the second for each measurement. In this example, a watch reading of 15.25 km at the first marker and 20.35 km at the second gives 5.10 watch kilometers. The segment covers 5.00 course kilometers.

How much would following 5:40/km on the watch cost?

At 5:40 per watch kilometer, covering 5.10 watch kilometers takes 1,734 seconds. That is 28:54 between the markers, or 34 seconds slower than 28:20. This comparison assumes the watch records the same distance for the segment. If the next segment has a different discrepancy, take fresh readings.

Can I use the watch's overall average instead?

The overall average combines every segment since the start. With 20.35 watch kilometers at the 20 km marker, the cumulative difference is 350 meters, while the segment difference is only 100 meters. Use the two marker readings to isolate the segment and compare its time with the chart.
