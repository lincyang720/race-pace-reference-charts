About treadmill pace

A common coaching rule of thumb adds roughly 15–20 seconds per mile (about 12 seconds per km) of effort for each 1% of incline versus flat outdoor running. This page applies that estimate so you can compare treadmill sessions with race goals from the race pace calculator.
