Pace conversion examples

  • 4:00 per kilometer ≈ 6:26 per mile ≈ 15.00 km/h (9.32 mph).
  • 5:00 per kilometer ≈ 8:03 per mile ≈ 12.00 km/h (7.46 mph).
  • 6:00 per kilometer ≈ 9:39 per mile ≈ 10.00 km/h (6.21 mph).

Limits of the marathon pace table

  • Grade: Flat-pace targets can be optimistic on uphills and conservative on runnable downhills.
  • Elevation gain: Repeated climbs can make the total effort implied by a flat pace too optimistic, even if the course finishes at its starting elevation.
  • Fatigue: If you slow late in the race, the steady-pace checkpoints can be optimistic.
  • Weather: Heat or a headwind can make the plan optimistic; a helpful tailwind can make it conservative.
  • Running tangents: Wide turns or weaving add distance, so the predicted time at the planned pace can be optimistic.

FAQ

How do I read this marathon split chart?

Find your goal time across the top. Follow that column down for the elapsed time from the start at each checkpoint.

How do I download the chart?

Choose Miles or Kilometers, then select Download CSV to export all checkpoints and goal columns for that view.

Why doesn't the chart predict my personal finish time?

Each column assumes a steady pace for a finish time you choose. Your race will depend on fitness, course and conditions. For a goal between columns, use the marathon pace calculator.
