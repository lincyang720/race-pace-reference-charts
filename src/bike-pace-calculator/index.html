How to calculate bike pace and cycling speed

A cycling pace calculator converts distance and elapsed time into two useful measurements: average speed in miles per hour (mph) or kilometers per hour (km/h), and bike pace in minutes per mile or kilometer. Average speed is the more common way to compare cycling rides; pace is useful when comparing travel time per unit of distance.

Bike speed and pace formulas

  • Average speed = distance ÷ elapsed time (for example, miles ÷ hours = mph).
  • Bike pace = elapsed time ÷ distance (minutes per mile or kilometer).
  • Estimated finish time = distance ÷ target speed. Use matching units: miles with mph, or kilometers with km/h.

Example: 25-mile cycling ride

If you cycle 25 miles in 1 hour 15 minutes, your average speed is 20 mph and your pace is 3:00 per mile. At the same average speed, a 50-mile ride would take about 2 hours 30 minutes. These are arithmetic estimates, not predictions of road conditions.

Bike pace calculator FAQ

How do I convert cycling pace to speed?

Divide 60 by your pace in minutes per mile to get mph, or divide 60 by minutes per kilometer to get km/h. For example, 3:00 per mile is 20 mph.

Can I calculate a bike race finish time?

Yes. Select Time from speed, enter your planned distance and target average speed, and the calculator estimates your moving time. Stops, wind, elevation, and fatigue can change the actual result.

Does this calculator work in kilometers?

Yes. Select Kilometers to enter ride distance in km and see speed in km/h and pace per kilometer.
